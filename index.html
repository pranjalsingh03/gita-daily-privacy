Privacy Policy

Gita Daily · Effective 7 October 2026

In short
  • Gita Daily does not collect, send or share any personal data.
  • There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.
  • The app has no internet permission; it works entirely offline.
  • Everything you enter stays on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

What the app stores, and where

To work, Gita Daily keeps the following on your device only, in the app's private storage:

  • the name you ask the app to call you;
  • your reading progress: verses marked as read, days you read, and time spent reading each day;
  • verses you save and reflection notes you write;
  • your settings: daily goal, reminder time and days, preferred translation and commentary, and text size.

This information never leaves your phone. We (the developer) cannot see it, and it is not sent to us or to anyone else. It may be included in your phone's own system backup if you have that turned on, under the control of your device and its platform provider.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your device. Reminders are created locally; no notification service or server is involved. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your phone's settings at any time.

Permissions

  • Notifications — to show your daily reminder, only if you turn it on.
  • Run at startup — to restore scheduled reminders after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration — used by the reminder notification.

The app does not request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage access.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Gita Daily, or clearing its storage in your phone's app settings, permanently deletes everything listed above. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Gita Daily is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, the policy and the app's store listing will say so before that happens.
